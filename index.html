Questions

Does it change anything in my Mozaik jobs?

No. Auto-Dim only reads the room you export to SketchUp. It writes its packet and its own backups into the job folder and never edits your Mozaik files.

Can I still edit the drawings?

Yes. The packet is a normal LayOut file. Add notes or dimensions by hand. When you re-run a room, your hand-added items are kept and flagged so you can check them against the new drawing.

Will it work with my Mozaik library?

It reads Mozaik's own room data, so most libraries work as-is. The beta exists to find the ones that don't. Every problem report makes it better for everyone.

Can I use my own title block?

The beta uses a clean standard template with your shop name, address and logo. Support for your own LayOut template is planned.

Does my job data get sent anywhere?

No. Auto-Dim runs entirely on your computer. It only sends something if you choose to email us a problem report.
